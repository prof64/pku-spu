Nidoran♂ (#032) and Nidorino (#033): what each surface is made of
Pokédex quotes are game text from PokeAPI's species data. "Art" is the Sugimori painting, "HOME" the Pokémon HOME render
(shape and color only). Row names and dials follow Nidoran♀ (#029) and Nidorina (#030).

NIDORAN♂ (#032)
Drawn surfaces: lilac body, dark spots, two big ears (teal inside), forehead horn, a crest of barbs on the back, white
claws, one incisor, red eye. No whiskers and no open mouth are drawn.

(25 pixels on forehead and ear rim) is kept in the hide row (KEEP_TOP): left out, it stayed pale on a head gone dark.

The palest lilac (brow, snout, foreleg) is kept in the hide row (KEEP_TOP) for the same reason as on Nidoran.

Calls the owner might overrule
- The male Nidoran's big back crest is Spines (0.4 / 0) like the female's barbs. Being solid, it is lit a step darker
  than the body under a low sun (field, back view). The art paints it like the ears, so Hide would also be defensible.
- Both horns stay Spines (0.4 / 0) although Nidorino's is "harder than a diamond": the art paints no highlight on it.
- Horn base is the hide's base color (Nidoran) or one step above it (Nidorino), not the palest lilac the front view
  paints it in: the art paints the horn body-colored, and with the palest shade as base it stood out pale under a lamp.
