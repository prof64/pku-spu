Machop line (#066 Machop, #067 Machoke): lighting evidence
Sources: Pokédex text from PokeAPI species data (English entries, game named); the Sugimori art and the
Pokémon HOME render in ../refs; the filed lighting of Machamp (#068), whose row names and dials are reused.
No Pokédex entry of either species says anything shines, glows or is wet, so nothing glows and all skin is matte.
