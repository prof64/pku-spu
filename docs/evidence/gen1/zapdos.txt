Departures and judgment calls
- The brown line (colors 6 and 5) around the yellow, the beak, the legs and the tufts is in the outline row, not each surface's darkest shade. The palette has no real step between yellow/orange and that brown (5 is only ever a softened line). Tried as shades first: olive blotches spread over the wings under the studio lamp and the legs and beak went dark brown in the field. As outline the lit sprite keeps the art's flat yellow; with the sun behind the line stays darker than the plumage (no bright rim).
- Wings left matte although Gold says "glittering wings" (Pidgeot got Glossy feathers 0.3 / 0 from "glossy feathers"). The painted art shows no sheen; glitter reads as sparks, not plumage. If overruled: set row "feathers" to finish 0.3, body 0.
