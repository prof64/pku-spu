Calls the owner might overrule
- The coat is Short fur for all three because nothing says fur or skin; matte either way.
- The brown pieces are Plated hide (Sandshrew's dials): no text says what they are; matte, dry, solid.
- The mustache is Whiskers (Nidoran's dials) although Bulbapedia calls Kadabra's "tufts of fur"; Fur at -0.1 is the alternative.
- Abra and Kadabra keep the brow patch as the coat's top shade; Alakazam's needed its own row.
