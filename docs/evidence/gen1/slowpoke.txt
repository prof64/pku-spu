CHOICES WORTH A SECOND LOOK
- Skin is Hide (0.25/0.08), not Turtle skin (0.5/0.08), though both are Water types: the art paints no highlight on either.
- KEEP_TOP on "hide" in both files: the palest pink is under 7% of the skin but is broad soft patches (forehead, back, knees); left out of the row it stayed as gray blots under the studio lamp and in the cave.
- Muzzle base is the middle cream (one step under the front view's lit shade): with the lightest as base, the back view's muzzle went brown in the field and near black in the cave.
- Mouth base is the dark inside (the color most of it is painted in), not the tongue.
- White eyes turn gray under the studio lamp and in the cave: line art is only tinted by the place's light (the approved Gastly does the same).
