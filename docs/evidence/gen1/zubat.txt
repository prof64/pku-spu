Zubat line: what each surface is made of, and why
Sources: Pokédex text from PokeAPI species data (every game, English); the Sugimori art (refs/art-41, art-42);
the Pokémon HOME renders (refs/home-41, home-42); the Black 2/White 2 sprites read pixel by pixel.
Nothing in either species gives off light: no glow anywhere.
