Notes
- "Balloon skin" is not in the standard table. Plain Hide (0.25 / 0.08) was tried on Koffing and looks nearly the same; the difference is a slightly brighter body with the sun behind it.
- A puff's three yellows are three rows (gas, gas pale middle, gas dark rim) with the same dials, because a row named gas gets no shape; this follows Gastly's cloud.
- Koffing's back is painted almost in one shade. Under a lamp from the side or in the cave, light splits it into two flat tones along a diagonal. Weezing's back, painted with more shades, does not.
- At rest (tick 0) Koffing's puffs are drawn tiny, so its sheet shows almost no gas; they were checked at tick 30.
