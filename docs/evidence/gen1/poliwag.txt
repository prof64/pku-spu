CHOICES TO KNOW ABOUT
- Base shade. Poliwhirl and Poliwrath paint the front in the light blue and the back almost wholly in the dark blue. The base is the blue between them (the shade Poliwag's count gives), not the most-used dark blue: with the dark blue the face came out a step dark under the studio lamp and in the field. Same reasoning for the white hands (white in front, gray from behind: base the pale gray between) and Poliwhirl's belly (white base turned its lower half dark olive in the field).
- The pale blue highlight color (head top, feet) is rare, so the tool leaves it as painted glints; keeping it in the row (KEEP_TOP) spread hard pale patches over the backs.
- The backs are painted in one flat blue, so a lamp or the cave puts a few soft-edged steps across them (the same as on Machamp's back).
