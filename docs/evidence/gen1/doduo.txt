Doduo line: evidence for every surface
======================================
Pokedex text is game text from PokeAPI (pokemon-species 84 and 85). "Bulbapedia" is the Biology section of each
page, read from Internet Archive snapshots of 2025-12-31 (Doduo) and 2026-01-03 (Dodrio). "Art" is the Sugimori
artwork (refs/art-84.png, art-85.png); "HOME" the Pokemon HOME render (shape and color only).
No Pokedex entry of either species names a material or says that any part shines or glows, so nothing glows and
every dial is the standard table's value for what the part is. Related finished files followed: 0021-spearow,
0022-fearow, 0016-pidgey (row names "feathers", "feathers, <part>", "beak", "dry hide, legs", "claws and teeth",
"eye (line art)", "mouth", and their dials).

Departures from the starting table: none. New row names, standard dials: "dry hide, black necks", "dry hide, necks",
"feathers, black", "feathers, tail", "feathers, tail tips".
Calls the owner might overrule:
- the down as Feathers (matte) rather than Fluff (-1 / 0.2);
- the necks as bare Dry hide rather than feathers (nothing official says which; the dials differ only slightly);
- Dodrio's "smooth layer of black feathers" kept matte, not Glossy feathers.
