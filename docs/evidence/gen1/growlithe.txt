Notes on the rows

  gone dark with the sun behind.

  darker cream, which most of both views is painted in. Under the studio lamp Arcanine's mane therefore
  shows that darker cream, not the art's paler highlights.
- Black wider than one pixel on body and legs is stripe; one-pixel black strokes there stay the drawn line.
