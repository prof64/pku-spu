Tangela (#114) and Ditto (#132): what each surface is and why
Pokédex quotes are game text from PokeAPI's species data (the game is named). "Art" is the Sugimori painting
(refs/art-114, art-132), "HOME" the Pokémon HOME render (shape and color only). Dials are finish / body.
Nothing on either glows: no entry says any part shines or gives off light.

## Tangela (#114)

Base shades: the vines' base is the mid blue (color 3: 1301 pixels, against 540 of the lighter one, both views).
The palest blue (the streak) is kept as the row's top shade: it is 163 pixels, not a rare glint.

## Ditto (#132)

Surface | Made of | Material | Evidence | Confidence
---|---|---|---|---
Body (lilac, everything) | one soft mass of cells; never described further | soft body 0.3 / 0.1 (a new row name with Frog skin's dials: "smooth, a faint sheen, not wet") | Black 2/White 2: "It can reconstitute its entire cellular structure to change into what it sees, but it returns to normal when it relaxes." Ruby/Sapphire: "DITTO rearranges its cell structure to transform itself into other shapes." No entry in any game calls it slime, jelly, goo, wet or see-through (all English entries read). Art: flat lilac, soft shading, opaque, with one small pale dot of light on top of each bump: a faint sheen at most. HOME: smooth, soft, opaque. So not Sludge (0.7 / 0.2) and not Jelly (0.6 / 0.45): both need a source that says wet or clear, and there is none. | opaque and not wet: high; the sheen dial: medium
Pale patch on top of each bump (front and back) | the same body, the art's dots of light | soft body, highlight (same dials, own row, one step up the body's colors) | Art paints those dots. Dark purple line on the lit side | drawn line | outline | The palette has no step between that purple and the body's shadow color; as the body's darkest shade it put a dark blotch across the back in a cave (the brief's pale-coat rule). | medium
Mouth | a drawn line | outline | Art: a pink smile line, shut; HOME paints a pink open mouth. The sprite draws only a black line with soft ends, no pink. | high (for this sprite)

Base shade: the body's base is its shadow color (color 6): the back view is painted almost wholly in it.

## Calls the owner might overrule
- Tangela's vines are "hairy vines" (-0.1 / 0.1) from the Platinum/Black/White entry; the art alone would give a plain matte plant body (0.1 / 0.1). Lit, the two look nearly the same.
- Ditto is opaque soft skin (0.3 / 0.1), not sludge or jelly like Grimer. In the rain column (a lamp close by) it still shows one broad highlight on the front, as other 0.3 skins do (Poliwag).
