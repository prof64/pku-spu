== Open call (Jigglypuff) ==
With the rules followed as written, Jigglypuff's front goes one shade darker and speckled in the field: its face is
painted in 13, which sits in the middle of the coat's ramp (base 14 below it, the highlight 12 above it), and the
short fur's roughness scatters the two shades. Tried and rejected: base 13 (clean front, but the back goes dark and
muddy in the field); the highlight left out of the row (it turns gray under the studio lamp and stays pale in the cave).
An alternative that looks clean in the field and under the lamp is in alt-highlight-row/: the highlight color in its
own row "short fur, highlights" with the coat's dials. It is not the delivered file because no finished sprite splits
a highlight into its own row, and under the rain lamp it washes out a little more around the eye.
