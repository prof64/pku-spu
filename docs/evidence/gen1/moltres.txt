Choices made on this sprite:
- Feathers base is the ochre (4), not the yellow (3): front is painted about half and half, the back almost all in 4 (both views: 4 on about twice as many pixels). Under a front lamp the front view's head therefore reads a step more orange than the art. Base 3 was tried: barely different, so the counting rule was kept.
- KEEP_TOP for feathers: the pale yellow (2) is under 7% of the plumage, so the tool dropped it; it then stayed as a pale oval on the body in the cave. It is the plumage's lit top (a broad patch on the body, a band along each wing), not a glint, so it is kept in the row.
- Flame base is the red (6), which most of the fire is painted in (both views), where Ponyta's row takes its second shade. Fire then never goes darker than its red.
- Nothing but the fire glows. The fire is most of the sprite, so it lights the cave strongly: the entries above say it does.
- Animations untouched (Idle, front and back).
