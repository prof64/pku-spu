Pokédex quotes are game text from PokeAPI species data. "Art" is the Sugimori painting (refs/art-*.png);
"HOME" is the Pokémon HOME render, used for shape and color only.

## Calls

- Bone is a new row. docs/materials.md, Part 2 guide, lists "Bone 0.2 / 0.1" for Cubone's skull and Marowak. Finish
  0.2 is kept; body is 0, not 0.1, by the rule that hard things are solid (a pale hard surface that lets light
  through stays bright in the cave while the body goes dark).
- The skull's olive contour stays in the bone row as its darkest shades (the palette has a step between the pale gray
  and the dark olive, and Marowak's back view paints that olive broadly). So bone in shade turns olive-gray, most
  visibly on the back view in the field.
- The body is Hide, like the Nidoran line. Dry hide (0.05 / 0.03) would also fit the art, which shows no sheen.
