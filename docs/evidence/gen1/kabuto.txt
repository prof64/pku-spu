Open call: the Crystal entry names the eyes "on its back" (the black dots on the shell) as the ones that glow, but the art and
the sprite draw those plain black and draw the red pair luminous. The glow is on the red pair. It lifts the cave backdrop a
little (corner pixel 40,25,19 without, 53,31,29 with), as Sableye's eyes do.
Other calls: base of the shell is the mid brown (color 4), which most of both views together is painted in; the palest brown
(color 2, the lit top seen from behind, 4% of the shell) is kept as a shade (KEEP_TOP), as on Cloyster and Kingler.
