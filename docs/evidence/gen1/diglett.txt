Notes for both
- Hide's base is the mid brown (13): the front heads are mostly it; the backs are painted a step darker (14), with the dark brown (15) still under it, so they do not go black.
- Hide's palest brown (12) is the lit crown (a real shade, 17% of Dugtrio's hide), kept in the row in both sprites (KEEP_TOP) so it darkens in the cave.
- Rock's base is the mid olive (8), as Onix's is its mid gray: the light olive (7) is slightly ahead in what shows, but with it as base the shaded faces fell to the darkest olive in the field and the cave and the stones went blotchy.
- Animations, drawings, palettes and variants are unchanged from the sources.
