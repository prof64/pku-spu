Departures and calls
- Both: the fur's dark brown contour colors stay in the fur rows but are not among their shades (so light only tints
  them). The palettes have no step between the cream and that brown; with the brown as the darkest shade, every fur
  pixel one step off the light fell to dark brown and the whole ball went dark and speckled in daylight.
- Primeape: shackles are Metal (dull, 0.6 / -1) on the painted highlight alone; no Pokédex text.
- Brown hands/feet/tail tip (Mankey) and brown limbs (Primeape) are Hide (bare skin), not brown short fur.
- A row whose name contains "line" is taken for line art by Smeargle, so the face-line row is named "fur, face marks".
